    Questions

    Short answers.

    Do I need crypto or a wallet?
    No. Purse only reads public prices and fees. It never connects to a wallet and can't move money.
    What do I pay, and to whom?
    You pay Otari for the AI it runs, through your own key. A typical question costs about 2¢. Purse itself is free.
    Can it spend more than my limit?
    No. Before each step it checks that the step fits in what's left. If it might not, it stops and tells you.
    Is my key safe?
    It's kept in your browser. It's sent only with your own questions, used for that one request, and never saved or logged on our server. You can remove it any time.
    Why was my question blocked?
    Requests to move all funds, share a seed phrase, or hidden instructions inside the text are refused before anything runs. You aren't charged for a blocked request.
    Where do the answers come from?
    It searches the web, then checks the numbers with code. Open "Show the working" under an answer to see the sources.
    Is this financial advice?
    No. It gives facts and numbers, not advice. Prices and fees change by the minute, so check before you act.
    Which AI models does it use?
    Open-weight models from the Qwen family, run through Otari by Mozilla.ai. The code is open on GitHub.
